Three things I had to decide or can’t decide

1. Attribution (CORE-PBM-001). Pinball Map’s licence requires a surface showing their data to link to our location’s listing. Today the card always renders that link. In the new design an unlisted machine shows “Not listed on Pinball Map” with no link at all — while the Model row above it is still catalog data sourced from Pinball Map. That may put the unlisted case out of compliance. Cheapest fix: make “Not listed on Pinball Map” itself the link to our location page.
2. Row order. I put Model + the Pinball Map line above Owner, under a divider from the description. Owner could just as easily come first.
3. Two states you didn’t name. A machine with no catalog match at all (homebrew, or never matched) — does the Model row disappear, or read “not set”? And a machine we’ve never synced, where we can’t honestly claim listed or not.
