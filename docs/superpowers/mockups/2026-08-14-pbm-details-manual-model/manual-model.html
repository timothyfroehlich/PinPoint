No checkbox either way. PP-3bbr proposed a “Use Pinball Map’s data” checkbox, but the Model dropdown already has “Not on Pinball Map” in it — two controls for one decision, and they could disagree. The dropdown wins; the checkbox is dropped.
The one thing I can’t decide for you. Suppose someone hand-enters Bordertown / Homebrew / 2019, and months later Pinball Map adds Bordertown to their catalog and the machine gets matched to it. What happens to the typed values?

Overwrite — the catalog wins outright, matching today’s rule that these fields are “never trusted from the client”. Simple, one source of truth, and it silently discards what a person typed.

Fill blanks only — the catalog supplies anything left empty and never overwrites a typed value. Respects the human, but leaves the machine permanently disagreeing with the catalog with nothing on screen saying so.

Ask at match time — the match surfaces as a prompt rather than applying itself. Honest, and the most work; it also means auto-link can no longer silently do this one.

Validation I’ll just pick unless you object: year 1930–next year, title/manufacturer trimmed and capped at the same length limits the catalog mirror uses.
